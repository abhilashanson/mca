MASTEC (Association of MCA)

MASTEC is a forum of students and staff of MCA, which strives at the total personality development of students. It is a platform that helps the students of MCA to grow with technical skills and also helps in the overall development of the student. It is actively involved in conducting invited lectures, seminars, webinars, cultural and technical events for the students.

Key Objectives

  • To bridge the gap between academic learning and industry expectations through workshops, coding hackathons, technical symposiums, and interactive sessions with industry experts.
  • To foster innovation and creativity by encouraging students to build real-world software projects and open-source contributions.
  • To enhance leadership and teamwork through organizing technical fests and collaborative events.
  • To promote research and continuous learning in emerging fields like AI, Machine Learning, and Cloud Computing.
  • To build strong professional networks by connecting current students with successful MCA alumni.
